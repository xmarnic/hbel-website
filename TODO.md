# Local SEO & Community Presence — Honeybee Early Learning

Goal: make Honeybee Early Learning the obvious choice when a Cheyenne /
Laramie County parent searches for preschool, and build local awareness ahead
of the Spring 2027 opening.

## Search engine foundations

- [ ] **Google Search Console** — verify `honeybeelearning.kids` (DNS TXT
      record is easiest) and submit `https://honeybeelearning.kids/sitemap.xml`
- [ ] **Bing Webmaster Tools** — import the site from Search Console
      (covers Bing + DuckDuckGo)
- [ ] Check indexing a week after submitting: search `site:honeybeelearning.kids`
      and confirm the new titles/descriptions appear

## Map & directory listings (use identical NAP everywhere)

NAP = Name, Address, Phone. Use exactly:
**Honeybee Early Learning LLC, 5519 Canyon Rd, Cheyenne, WY 82009**
(plus melissa@honeybeelearning.kids and https://honeybeelearning.kids)

- [ ] **Google Business Profile** — category "Preschool", mark as opening
      Spring 2027, add photos of the space, hours, and a description with
      "home-based preschool in Cheyenne". This is what puts you in the map
      pack for "preschool near me" searches — highest-impact item on this list
- [ ] **Bing Places for Business** — mirrors Google Business Profile
- [ ] **Apple Business Connect** — gets you into Apple Maps
- [ ] **Yelp for Business** — free listing
- [ ] **Facebook Business Page** — doubles as a local discovery + parent
      communication channel
- [ ] **Nextdoor** — business page; huge for neighborhood word-of-mouth,
      especially for a home-based preschool
- [ ] Wyoming / Cheyenne child-care directories — the state licensing
      database and any local "find child care" portals once licensed
