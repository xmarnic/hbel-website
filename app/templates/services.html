Part-Time Preschool Classes in Cheyenne, Wyoming

Honeybee Early Learning is a home-based preschool program for children ages 3-5 in a mixed-age setting, welcoming families from Cheyenne and throughout Laramie County. With a small group size of 6 students to one teacher, your child gets personalized care and attention. We offer four flexible part-time schedules to fit your family's routine and needs.

Honeybee Early Learning uses The Peaceful Press literature and play-based curriculum. We believe children learn best through purposeful, play-based learning. Our program blends joyful, hands-on, theme-based activities with intentional discovery and structured explorations to nurture the whole child.

We plan to open our doors for our first classes in Fall 2027. Join our interest list to be notified as soon as enrollment opens.

When enrollment opens, here's what reserving your child's classroom slot will look like:

  • Completed Registration Form
  • Up-to-date immunization records
  • Enrollment Fee: A $25 non-refundable fee is due immediately upon submitting your enrollment application. (This will be used towards classroom preparation and administration processing.)
  • Security Deposit: A refundable tuition deposit equal to one month's tuition must be on file before your child's first day of class. This deposit is held to secure your spot and will be applied to your final month of care, provided proper withdrawal notice is given.

Full payment or agreed installments must be made as agreed at enrollment. Deposits are non-refundable, and all payments must be made within 90 days of the initial deposit. Cancellations must be made via email or over the phone.
